## Musing 227

The recent announcement of ArtCraft to reverse engineer Adobe products and reimplement them using AI is quite interesting.

It's probably the one place where even those more significantly anti-AI have been "yeah, that's fine" simply because of how awful
Adobe is. 

But I also think this is when we see the inflection point where there is stronger anti-AI response from the corporations.

Since now, there's no longer reason to use a product from a terrible company - you can just remake it yourself, for potentially far cheaper. 

Whish is theoretically a no-go, but when you also consider that game pirates *refused* to pirate games from good indie studios, 
is suggestive that people will make sacrifices for what they perceive to be justice to be done.
