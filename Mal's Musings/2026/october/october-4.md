## Musing 221

God's mercy and grace to us this year was shown in how we have had an unusually quiet hurricane season in the Atlantic Ocean.

Too often, I think our materialistic age leads us to forget that weather is an act of God. We think that because we understand 
many of the mechanics of the water cycle, that we are the masters of it. And yet God's providence is quick to bring us to heel 
when He so desires. 

While there is much to comment on how the Pacific is seeing a much busier storm season, I would only suggest we remember God's
providence in all things and praise Him for His wise rule in a rebellious world.
