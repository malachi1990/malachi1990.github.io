## Musing 241

God's providence often moves in surprising ways. Sometimes He directs us so forcefully that if it were a TTRPG, 
the players may complain about being railroaded. 

Other times God's providence makes us think we're doing A, but really being prepared for B (or even C/D/E). 

And one of the most annoying things (which teaches us our dependence on God) is this latter situation, especially when
God's shutting the door on A after we feel like He's been calling us to do A for a long time.
