## Musing 236

One of the things this year has exposed to me is how often my thoughts are repetitive, and how little I really have to say. 

Much of what I do say is not something I came up with originally, but is reflective of the influences on me. 

That, and too often the busyness of life kicks in and I don't have time to think deep thoughts like I used to.
