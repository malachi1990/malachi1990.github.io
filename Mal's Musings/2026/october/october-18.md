## Musing 234

As I look back on my life as I hit what's likely near the halfway point, there are some moments that from a worldly perspective 
were small, but have had immense impact on me. 

One is my father teaching me in the small moments. While he was ultimately an example of making shipwreck of your life through
pride, there were many small moments of wisdom about marriage, life, and how to handle other people that stay with me to this day.

There are so many moments that I look back on that I wish I could easily share with my child by letting them meet dear ol' 
granddad. But Lord willing I can give them wisdom at least as good.
