## Musing 228


I'm pretty sure that I've written on it before in other contexts, but one of the  patterns of God's judgement on sin is that
He lets either the sin itself become the judgement, or that we reap the consequences of our own actions become the judgement.

I was struck by that reading the passage when Israel sends the spies into the land, and because of their unbelief, are forbidden from entering.

And what do they do? Decide to, against Moses' warning that God would not be with them, attempt to enter the Promised Land anyways. When they promptly 
get destroyed.
