## Musing 242

I've noted before that God often brings about judgement through the consequences of our actions.

One of the examples I've used frequently is how WW1 was started because the network of alliances that should have prevented war from ever starting in turn became
the engine to create the biggest war in history to that point. 


So what will God's just judgement on this age, with its rampant selfishness and faith in politics or economics to solve 
all problems look like?

As AI is advancing and we see more and more its impact, I wonder if we're not already seeing the judgement begin. We see
the superabundance in Western society that technology promised, and yet we still have the poor, disease, famine, and 
every other problem imaginable.

What the failure of AI will be I don't know, but if it is a means of God's judgement upon us, I suspect we'll 
remember the thorns of it more than the blessings.
