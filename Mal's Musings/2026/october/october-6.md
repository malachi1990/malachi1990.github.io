## Musing 223

One of the things that has amazed me in the last 20 years is that absolute intellectual implosion of both the left and the right
in America. 

Even 10 years ago, both sides could consistently have leaders who had coherent thoughts that could extend beyond 2-3 sentences, 
regardless of whether they were talking heads, or those who were behind the scenes (or even, on occasion, actual political leaders!).

I don't pretend to know all the reasons why such degradation happened and so quickly, but all that echoes in my mind is "professing
to be wise, they became fools", which rings especially true when the first thing both sides do now is denigrate the intelligence
of the other side, giving the implicit "if you were really smart, you'd agree with me!"
