## Musing 240

One of the things I do not look forward to on judgement day is how many people (myself included) will hve to give account 
of their idle words.

Especially with the internet making it so easy  to interject ourselves or showcase our confident ignorance(and such displays
of ignorance are ever more painful to read), we will all have words that will have done no one good. 

Ultimately, this is why we are dependent on Jesus for all things, since Christ is the Word.
