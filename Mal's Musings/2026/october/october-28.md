## Musing 244

There is a part of me that hates how much I think about AI. 

On the one hand, it's a phenomenal tool, and I expect it to continue improving.

On the other . . . even though it still can't really replace me at my job (it for sure can do the coding part, but 
that's the main part it can do), I don't really make that choice. My manager/director does. And so it really depends on 
their thoughts about the matter.

And that exposes that I want to be in control. And I'm not. God is. And that anxiety around me keeping my job, while
well understood, also shows where I have to grow in my trusting of God's wise providence.
