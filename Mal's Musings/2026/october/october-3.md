## Musing 220


I don't know what it is, but even though I know the news cycle *has* to hype the AI stuff, it still spikes my anxiety so
much to see that news cycle. 

While yes, AI is impressive, there are hard limits to what the current architecture can do. The recent attempt to solve the 
Riemann hypothesis which failed (despite making some progress) showcases this fairly clearly. Despite using agents to "talk"
to one another, and doing some work that is admittedly extremely impressive, it couldn't solve a problem that likely needs 
fundamentally new tools to solve.

The terrifying part though, is that most work is not innovation, but recombination. While specific tasks might be new to us, 
they're not fundamentally new in the grand scheme of things.
