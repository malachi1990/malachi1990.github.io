## Musing 203

Every generation has a memory of some life altering event. My generation's was 9/11.

I was in 6th grade English. And the wide range of emotions and its impact on society cannot be understated. For so many of us, 
seeing 3000 lives ended in just a short time became a core memory.  There is much evil in the world, and may we oppose evil
with good.

But in the following days, we saw overall unity among a wide swath of Americans. But the cracks in the superficial unity were already
forming, and 25 years later, we are ever more divided, whether by politics, race, class, religion, or any other number of dividing lines.

Which points to a reality - fealty to an earthly kingdom isn't sufficient to bring true unity. We need a greater reality 
and foundation to be joined to in order to find true unity. And that can only be found in Jesus, who while promising peace, also 
promises to unite those who follow Him. And anyone who has suddenly found a providential encounter with a brother can attest 
to the deep sense of unity that can be found in Christ.
