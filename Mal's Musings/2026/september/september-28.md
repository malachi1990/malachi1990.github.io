## Musing 216

There is a great pleasure in rereading good books. From better appreciating the foreshadowing or picking up on details previously
missed, a good book has its rewards for re-reading.

And yet, even the best books written by mortal hands eventually runs out of details for us to notice, no more missed foreshadowing
for us to appreciate. While that may not dull our appreciation of said work, there is a sadness that first re-read when nothing
new turns up.

By contrast, Scripture never fails to turn up something new. While mere men did write it, there was an infinite God behind them. And because 
God in His infinitude is expressed there, there will never come a day when Scripture fails to give us something new, if we 
have ears to hear.
