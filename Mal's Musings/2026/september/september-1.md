## Musing 194

One of the hard things about knowing a lot is that it can be easy to get discouraged at correcting the same errors over and over again.

When I go through facebook or other social media and see the same attacks on reformed theology, it gets annoying, especially
when it's such a blatant misrepresentation.

Which brings to mind one of the things I learned from Dr. James White when he talked about why he did debate - it wasn't for the person
he was debating, but for those from whom he may never hear from about the encouragement they received, and because ultimately
we don't give account to man, but to God. 

And God will ask us why were or were not honest and faithful in our calling.
