## Musing 215

Need to start writing on Sundays to account for there being no leeway left. 

I'm contributing to a conversation on Reformed Theology, and it struck me that John 10 is the basis for assurance in evangelism.

In John 10, Jesus says that His sheep hear His voice and that they come when He calls. With that in mind, we should be confident
in our evangelism, because Jesus knows His sheep and He calls them. Even if we ourselves do not directly see conversions (
though we should always pray for it!) that Jesus knows who His sheep are and that He is gathering them comforts us because
He will succeed despite whatever failures we have.
