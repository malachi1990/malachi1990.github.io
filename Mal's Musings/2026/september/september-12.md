## Musing 203

In re-reading the Didache, on the one hand, I *love* the confirmation that the saints met *daily* with one another. 

Such relationships are a testament to the radical power the gospel has.

On the other, I am an introvert. The idea of meeting my workmates on video conference is annoying. And so the idea of meeting
with others, believers though they may be, is difficult.

But such is also a place where I must die to self in order to follow Christ.
