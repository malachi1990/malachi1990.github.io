## Musing 199

As I get older, the sayings in Proverbs warning against laziness make more and more sense.

It seems like taking what should be a 5-minute break seems to more and more become a half hour or longer detour (sometimes
for legit reasons, sometimes not), and as the day flies by, it can feel like the important things didn't get done, even though
the day was objectively busy or had necessary things get done.

I hope in time that discipline can help me make better use of the time.
