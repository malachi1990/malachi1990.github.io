## Musing 214

Spent an hour weeding, and I am reminded how gardening can be such a good metaphor for the spiritual life. 

Take care of weeding can be like dealing with sin. Sin can give the appearance of life, but unless you specifically deal with it, 
it can survive normal maintenance, get everywhere before you realize it, and be a pain in the neck to deal with once you 
realize you need to deal with it.
