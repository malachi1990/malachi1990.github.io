## Musing 207


I've been dealing with a situation where I'm a mod in a community, and there was a situation where honestly. . . the
number of screw-ups (including my own) that occurred exceeds what can be counted on both hands.

Which makes it hard and uncomfortable to be the one to dispense justice. Because I know experientially that "I screwed up too, 
and I'm not receiving punishment" and so I want to show mercy as well.

And while there are times such may be appropriate, there are times when we have to live with that split. And its deeply uncomfortable. 

In part because dispensing justice is already not fun, and then there's the rightful pushback of "well, you screwed up too!"

And yet it's a reminder there will be a day when perfect justice is rendered. When either we will receive just punishment for our sins, 
or mercy and undeserved forgivness through Jesus Christ. 

May that thought remind us not to waste the short (and ever shortening) time we have in this world.
