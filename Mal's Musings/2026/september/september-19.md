## Musing 209

One of the sources of entertaining tv shows as a kid I'd watch with my dad were the shows mocking criminals for being dumb.

And one of the things he taught me was "if you're gonna be dumb, be ok with getting laughed at", because people will mock
stupidity, both because it's hilarious, and because often times we go "well, at least I'm not that dumb".

Add on the shame of hearing the commentary who screwed up worse than you, and the shame is likely enough on its own to make people not want
to do stupid crimes.

As the world's insanity seems to spiral ever more, I sometimes think we need to make it mandatory to watch people who do stupid 
stuff and mock them, in the hopes that the return of shame will at least slow down the rampant criminality in this day.

Of course, the restraint of evil by society and the gov't is only a bandaid to the spiritual problem that underlies criminality, but
man, do I get nostalgia at such shows.
