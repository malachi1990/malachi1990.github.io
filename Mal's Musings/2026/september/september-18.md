## Musing 208

I don't know what happened with the AI news cycle. Either we're truly moving into the next phase of the AI bubble (when the
constant news no longer helps, causing the money to dry up), or people are just getting bored with the technology, or are waking
up to the legitimate dangers of AI, I don't know.

On the one hand, the recent mathematical progress is impressive, with the ethical issues equally distressing. 

As AI advances and hits its limitations, it'll be interesting to see what ends up happening. I will strive to remember God's sovereignty 
and that His will is what will be done.
