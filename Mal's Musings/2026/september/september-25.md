## Musing 213

This week has been a battle of discouragement at work, which in turn has been a reminder of how sorrow and discouragement wear on the soul.

We know that work is difficult, and there is a pleasure in taking something difficult and making it easy. 

But such is also a reminder that in this world, sin's impact can even be felt in the mundane. Work was not supposed to be difficult, and yet it is.
