## Musing 265

In the AI news, the Riemann hypothesis has been solved!

On the one hand, that is epic news. One of the oldest open math problem can now be closed. 

On the other . ..  no one knows if it's actually been solved. It's such a massive proof that humans
will take a while to fully understand it.

It'll be interesting to see if the proof showcases genuinely new techniques (representing a breakthrough
in itself) or if it's a combination of nothing but existing techniques, where the novelty is the combination and the scale of things chained together.
