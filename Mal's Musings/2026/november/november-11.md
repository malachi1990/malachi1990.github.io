## Musing 256

There is much to said about the insanity of our day. And all of it is heart-breaking. 

Like Lot, I am troubled by the evil our society tolerates and demands be made normal. And while I eagerly await the day 
when God makes all things right and brings an end to sin, that doesn't take away the pain sin brings.

Sin is intoxicating and destructive. And as society seems on one hand, hellbent on its own destruction through the throwing
off of Christian morality, it also desperately seeks answers for the pain from sin. The AI worshippers cling to the hope
that AI will solve the problem of sin. And while the super abundance in the West has made sin hurt less, that hurt is still there
and we should not expect otherwise. 

For others, gov't provides the answer. Whether that be through social safety nets or the punishment of sin, there is hope 
that mere man can fully stop the problems. And Romans 13 makes it clear the purpose of gov't is to restrain sin via punishing it.
But that is a bandaid on the deeper problem - men are sinners. And because we're sinners, we sin. And we love it. 

We need a solution that deals with this. We must be born again into something new.
