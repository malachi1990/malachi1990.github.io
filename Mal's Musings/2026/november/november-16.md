## Musing 261

Having completed Numbers in the annual reading through the Scriptures, I was once again struck by 
how futile the Levitical sacrifical system was. There were so many sacrifices (making 1 year old 
blemishless animals a prime import I'm sure!), and yet it was never enough. 

The blood of bulls and goats was never sufficient to bridge the gap between us and God. And yet 
Jesus was able to do so. 

Too often we don't feel that distance. While I'd like to say that it is our uniquely post-modern attitude
to blame, Paul had the right answer for why - sin. In our suppression of the truth of who God is, we either have to elevate ourselves or bring God down to our level, and oftentimes it's both in one fell
swoop. 

Let us destroy every thought in our lives that would encourage us to do so and instead magnify God and 
humble ourselves that we may remember that the gap between us is far larger than we can ever bridge on our own. We needed Jesus to bridge that gap for us. The rivers of blood sacrifices couldn't do it, yet
modern man in his sin often thinks he needs to pay nothing for the heinousness of his sin.
