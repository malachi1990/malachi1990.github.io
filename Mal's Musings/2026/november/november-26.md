## Musing 270

On this day of thanks, it is a reminder that all we have is a gift from God. 

Which helps the anxiety at the societal turmoil, and the whole "AI will take your job" thing. 

God will provide. Sometimes that provision looks different than I'd like, especially when it pushes my trust in Him.

But I am thankful for the many blessing He's provided. A consistent job, family, house, health (and the ability to improve it!), among so many other things. Truly I could go all day as I get more and more specific. 

But it all comes back to - all I have is from God, and He has been generous. May my heart never forget the gratitude He is owed.
