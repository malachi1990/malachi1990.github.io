## Musing 254

One of the clearest signs the Republicans haven't yet learned their lesson is that instead of asking themselves "How do
I win in these times" is that after a moment of shock, they go back to the tried and true playbook of "well, the other
team cheated, so I must be right!"

After spending years of facing accusations (sometimes even being honest about it too!) gerrymandering districts to favor 
Republicans, years of implementing stricter election security, and saying "well, the Democrats don't like it because it
just means they can't cheat, so they'll never actually win!" 

The sad part is that it exposes how many people who profess Christ love lies. Because instead of holding the Republicans
accountable for bad candidates/plaftorms resulting in near historic losses in heavily Republican states (many of whom 
were among the most enthusiastic in the gerrymandering and stricter protocols), said professing Christians believe the lies
and parrot them unquestioningly.

And we wonder why repentance seems scarce in our day.
