## Musing 259

All false teachings is worth despising, but one of the more insidious ones is that of conditional salvation.

Without fail this particular false teaching destroys the notion that God is a good father. Because the
god of conditional salvation doesn't discipline them. He only casts them out.

And secondarily, this view also without fail minimizes sin to its externality (and thus, sometimes results in sinless perfectionism) and fails to acknowledge the depth of internal sin.

I weep for those whose god is such a bad father.
