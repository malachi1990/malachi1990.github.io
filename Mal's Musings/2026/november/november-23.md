## Musing 267

It has been fascinating to watch the implosion of the Riemann hypothesis proof from "oh wow, what is this" to "well, it compiles in Lean, so it must be good!" to "oh wow, this proof is junk". 

Not to mention all the other "proofs" that take the original and strip it down and try to maintain the proof. 

And now more and more people calling out structural problems. 

The times are certainly interesting.
