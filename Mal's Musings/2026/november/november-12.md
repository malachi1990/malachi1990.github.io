## Musing 257

One of the things this exercise has helped with is my procrastination habits. The ease with which I 
handled school growing up meant it took until I was nearly an adult to really start hitting situations
where I couldn't just put something off to the last minute and get away with it.

Having said that, I will not miss this exercise. And while I won't meet all my goals this year, I am still glad to have done this.
