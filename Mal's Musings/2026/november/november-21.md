## Musing 266

Our lives have a number of eras in them. And with them, times of transition. Over the last few years, I've experienced a number of transitions. From being single to married, to having a child. 

Each of those era of life have import and meaning because of God's providential ordering, even if I never discover exactly what those purposes were and are.

Ultimately, it's all for my conforming to the image of Christ, but there are oftentimes inscrutable workings in God's providence. And as God is far wiser than I, such should be expected.
